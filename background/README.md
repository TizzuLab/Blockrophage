# Background

`macrophage_biology.md` is the background reading for the course: the lecturer's
condensation of the Immunology III lecture on tissue macrophages and the tissue
repair lectures, with the history of the ideas the experiments test and questions
at the end of every section. `reading_list.md` holds the references and the two
public datasets used in the dry lab.

link]`, not in this repository, because they contain third-party figures.
